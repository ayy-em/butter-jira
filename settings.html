Team roster
Who counts as "the team": drives the Team Only filter, display-name overrides, and later standup and planning. Stored on this device only — it holds colleagues' details, so it never goes into synced storage or a config export unless you tick the box below.
Search needs the "Browse users" permission in Jira. If your account doesn't have it, add people by account ID (from the Jira profile URL) or by email — an email is linked automatically the next time that person shows up in a harvest.
Field mapping
Custom field IDs differ per Jira site. Discover resolves them by field name; override manually if your site uses different names.
Monitoring checks
Hygiene checks shown on the Monitor tab. All are on by default; untick to mute one. Every check skips epics and anything already done, and all but "unassigned" also skip sub-tasks — a sub-task hangs off a story, so it has no epic of its own and inherits its parent's dates and estimate.
Additional fields
Extra field IDs requested on every issue query, comma-separated (e.g. customfield_10050, timetracking). Entries from config.local.json are merged in and shown below.
Branding (optional)
Files under assets/brand/ are untracked by git.
Backup & transfer
Export writes a JSON file with your site, boards, status groups, field mapping and branding. Import merges a file back in — use it to move to another browser or hand a working setup to a teammate.
